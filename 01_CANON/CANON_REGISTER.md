# W.I.S.D.O.M. Canon Register

This file records established project direction.

## C-001 — Learner-centered product direction

W.I.S.D.O.M. is centered on a premium beginner course for people who want to make AI work for their own goals.

It is not primarily a coding bootcamp or career-training program.

Professional and advanced engineering pathways may overlap with the course, but they are not the sole definition of its audience.

## C-002 — Respect for the learner

All W.I.S.D.O.M. instruction, marketing, and presentation must be grounded in respect for the learner.

The project must avoid pandering, condescension, and elitism.

## C-003 — Evidence-led development

Product, market, and curriculum decisions should be based on investigated facts and demonstrated evidence where reasonably possible rather than treating untested claims as truth.

## C-004 — Theory and practice are distinct instructional concerns

Conceptual understanding and practical action should be deliberately connected while remaining distinguishable.


## C-005 — Research and brainstorming status lifecycle

W.I.S.D.O.M. classifies research, brainstorming, design claims, and candidate project direction as **Theory**, **Provisional**, **Probational**, or **Canon**.

- **Theory** records an explanatory idea, hypothesis, or conceptual possibility not yet strong enough for candidate adoption.
- **Provisional** records a candidate that has survived meaningful initial challenge but remains unproven or unresolved.
- **Probational** records a candidate deliberately being used, trialed, or observed to gather evidence.
- **Canon** records direction explicitly adopted by the Operator.

Brainstorming is noncanonical by default.

Only an explicit Operator decision promotes material into Canon. Repetition, implementation, repository placement, successful testing, or agent confidence are not substitutes for adoption.

## C-006 — Explicit elevation confirmation

When the Operator explicitly requests elevation of material between W.I.S.D.O.M. status categories, the coordinator must first present a concise promotion summary covering:

- what is being promoted;
- current and target status;
- what survived falsification or supports the promotion;
- what was rejected or contradicted;
- unresolved risks;
- the exact scope proposed for promotion.

The status change occurs only after explicit Operator confirmation.

This does not revoke the coordinator's standing ability under C-005 to classify ordinary brainstorming as Theory or Provisional. It applies when an elevation is explicitly being considered or requested.

Silence is not a substitute for confirmation.
