# GAB and T.C.G.L. — Integrated governance research

Checkpoint · 2026-09-08 · Theory / nonbinding research design

## Current user clarification

Goal: maximum safe autonomy with minimum necessary supervision. Governance should enable long stretches of useful agent work and surface the decisions genuinely reserved to the user.

GAB (Governance Autonomy Boundary) is a working term for the recurring region where deterministic controls, explicit policy, and instructions leave legitimate choices requiring inference. Inference is not automatically a reason to ask the user.

Autonomy selects among authorized actions. Governance authority changes permissions or governing requirements. Research must study deterministic enforcement, instructional governance, bounded inference, recommendations, and user decisions throughout project/workflow/task/subtask/action levels.

TCGL meanings: Theory is the idea pool; Provisional curates and investigates without operational implementation; Probational implements a bounded trial under observation; Canon is the amendable governing default after sufficient evidence and approval.

The user expressly permits agents to promote Theory → Provisional with a record of reason, source task, expected benefits/consequences, evidence, uncertainty, and scope. This does not expand resources, task scope, or operational authority.

**Provisional → Probational requires explicit user authorization.** Agents may prepare and recommend a trial, not make this transition themselves. The earlier proposal for agent-initiated trials under generic experiment envelopes is superseded. Executing a specifically user-authorized trial is distinct from deciding to adopt a new candidate.

The user did not thereby grant all backward transitions, rejection powers, self-renewal, or canonicalization. Agents contribute evidence and recommendations; actual governance changes remain with the appropriate authorized party.

## Findings captured before further analysis

- Distinguish uncertainty about what to do from uncertainty about permission. Only some uncertainty requires a user decision.
- The five routing categories can cooperate on the same action; they are not five mutually exclusive owners.
- A deterministic mechanism enforces adopted rules but can still encode the wrong rule.
- Distributed agent observations may be useful, biased, correlated, or incomplete. Preserve source evidence and disagreement.
- Count missed required escalations and total review/rework effort alongside unnecessary interruptions.
- A documented user gate can coexist with autonomous execution of the approved plan; do not re-request unchanged authority for every trial action.
- Preserve historical policy proposals but remove them from the current recommendation path when the user changes direction.
