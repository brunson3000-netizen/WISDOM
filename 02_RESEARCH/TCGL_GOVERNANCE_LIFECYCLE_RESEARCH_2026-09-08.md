## 2. Definitions preserved

| Stage | Meaning | What it does not supply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theory | An idea under reasoning, without implementation status or authority. | Permission to apply it as policy. |
| Provisional | Promising enough for deliberate investigation of consequences, applications, risks, conflicts, and possible implementation. It is not implemented. | A trial disguised as observation. |
| Probational | Deliberately implemented in a bounded experiment and observed in practice. | Permanence or a wider scope. |
| Canon | Evidence-supported and explicitly approved durable governing policy, amendable through the applicable process. | Universal truth, immunity from review, or authority in another project. |

Provisional means investigation before implementation. Probational means observation during implementation. These meanings supersede the first installment's looser preservation/monitoring formulation. Ordinary note capture need not be promoted just to be retained.

A candidate tested in a sandbox is implemented within that sandbox; isolation does not make the implementation Provisional. A written design or consequence analysis can remain Provisional. A simulation that actually applies the candidate as a decision rule is a bounded trial in its test environment. Its results do not establish production readiness.

For regression, “not implemented” describes the current operating state, not erasure of trial history. A return to Provisional retains prior implementation receipts and failure evidence.

## 4. Core model: three linked records

Attach maturity to **principle ID + exact revision + scope**, not to a catchy name.

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Knowledge record | Claim, stage, scope, evidence, counterevidence, uncertainty, and investigation history. |
| Authorization record | Who may perform which transition or action, under what parent authority, constraints, resources, and expiration. |
| Activation record | What revision actually ran, where, when, under which grant, with what outcome and shutdown/recovery state. |

Supporting operational fields are not extra TCGL maturity stages. Examples: approved-but-not-started, running, stopped, expired, or recovery-failed. “Rejected” is a recorded disposition, preserving the prior stage and reasons.

An approved trial remains Provisional with approval pending execution until its implementation is confirmed. A failed start does not earn Probational status. A partially applied trial is an incident requiring truthful partial-activation recording and containment, not a clean “not started.”

A stopped Probational trial remains historically Probational with stopped status until an authorized disposition. Expiration ends permission; it does not imply the hypothesis was disproved.

A Canon amendment has a new candidate revision. The existing canon remains effective until the proper amendment or suspension process changes it. Researching its replacement does not repeal it.

## 5. Proposed authority and transition protocol

All delegated powers in this section are proposals.

| Movement | Evidence/decision needed | Proposed authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theory → Provisional | Task relevance; investigateable claim; reason; scope; available evidence and unknowns. | Assigned agent may classify within an adopted task research allowance. No new tools, budget, or operational rule. |
| Provisional → Probational | Specific testable revision, trial plan, current valid grant, ready controls/telemetry/recovery, and actual activation receipt. | Named approver, or agent acting inside an explicitly approved experiment envelope. |
| Probational → Canon | Operational evidence, limitations, impact on existing policy, review, and durable adoption decision. | WISDOM Operator under existing repository rules; no agent canonicalization inferred. |
| Provisional → Theory / Rejected | Findings no longer justify structured attention. | Agent may recommend; adopted classification delegation could permit disposition within its own scope. Never delete contrary evidence. |
| Probational → Provisional | Trial stopped and recovery state verified; redesign/investigation rationale. | Stop/recovery authority and classification authority checked separately. Failed recovery remains visibly unresolved. |
| Probational → revised Probational | New revision, new comparison, and a grant that explicitly covers the variation. | No reuse of old approval or success by default. If outside the envelope, return to review before activation. |
| Canon → reconsideration | Material contradictory evidence or changed assumptions. | Any assigned researcher may raise findings. Suspension/amendment follows existing authority. |
| Any scope → broader/different scope | Applicability analysis and target authority. | Create a linked scope-specific record; do not overwrite the source's status or copy its grant. |

Scope is a set of tasks, resources, affected people, actions, and conditions. Labels such as task/workflow/project summarize it; scopes may overlap without forming a neat hierarchy. Permission in one scope does not automatically cover another.

**Activation gate:** valid actor and grant; exact candidate revision; scope and effects covered; no conflict with higher policy; resource limits available; evidence capture ready; stop/recovery plan available; no revocation or expiration. Unknown gate result means no activation.

Check again at the action boundary. Queued work cannot rely indefinitely on admission-time permission. Delegation may narrow existing authority; TCGL cannot create authority the parent lacks.

## 6. Agent recognition without task hijacking

Recognition should be cheap and tied to the owned task. Capture a concise observation at a natural checkpoint; deduplicate against existing records; investigate only within authorized attention and resource limits. Finding a promising idea does not create an assignment to pursue it.

| Signal | What it may justify | What could defeat the inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repeated intervention or ambiguity | Investigate an unclear task or authority rule. | Different tasks, useful clarifications, or changed operator preferences. |
| Fewer failures under a practice | Investigate a candidate explanation. | Easier cases, tool/model changes, selective reporting. |
| Lower compute or reasoning burden | Compare total cost and quality. | More hidden rework or poorer outcomes. |
| Apparent failure prevention | Define a testable prevention hypothesis. | The prevented event is counterfactual and may never have happened. |
| Useful across tasks | Investigate transfer. | Tasks share the same narrow conditions or copied evidence. |
| Contradictory results | Narrow scope, redesign, or reject. | Neither majority vote nor repetition resolves the cause. |

“Repeated usefulness” must come from honestly described existing observations or authorized trials. It must not reward the agent for secretly applying a Provisional policy until it seems successful.

Distinguish observation, interpretation, hypothesis, and recommendation. Confidence includes an explanation of uncertainty; a model's numeric confidence is not a calibrated probability unless validated.

Preserving a note is ordinary research work. Promoting its investigative priority can affect time and attention even though it creates no policy authority. This is why Theory → Provisional is low consequence, not consequence-free.

## 7. Proposed experimentation envelope

An envelope must be issued by an authority that can grant every covered effect. It should name eligible actors/tasks, permitted types of policy variation, resource boundaries, a total trial budget, concurrency limits, expiration, stop triggers, evidence rules, and a tested recovery method.

Initial recommendation: permit experiments only within disposable, bounded resources; exclude new spending authority, credential/security changes, external irreversible effects, changes to the TCGL promotion machinery itself, and changes to evidence or acceptance rules during the test. Existing resource authorization still applies to every call.

“Safe” is the conclusion of checking defined limits, not an agent-selected label. Envelopes that overlap need a composition check: two individually bounded experiments can interfere or exceed a shared limit.

Record activation before admitting trial work where possible, then record the result of activation. Handle crashes and partial effects through reconciliation. An implementation cannot rely on logging alone: actual action mediation is needed, and completeness depends on covering every relevant execution path.

On expiration, revocation, missing required evidence, or a stop trigger: stop admitting new experimental effects. Handle in-flight work under the predeclared drain/cancel procedure. Restore baseline only when recovery is authorized and its preconditions still hold; otherwise contain and escalate. Never label recovery complete merely because the maturity field changed.

## 8. Evidence, attribution, and promotion criteria

Minimal human-facing card: idea and scope; why investigate; evidence and counterevidence; current stage; next decision and owner. Expand only when implementation is proposed.

Machine-facing design:

| Record | Fields needed when applicable |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate | ID, revision/hash, exact claim, source task, scope, maturity, disposition, evidence links, counterevidence, uncertainty. |
| Decision | Unique event ID, expected prior revision/state, proposed change, actor, authority reference, reason, timestamp. |
| Trial | Candidate revision, environment/task/model/tool/instruction identities, baseline, hypothesis, measures, limits, grant, start/end and stop plan. |
| Outcome | Attempts and failures retained, intervention reasons, authorization decisions, execution receipts, quality checks, recovery result. |
| Adoption | Exact scope/version, approver, supporting evidence, impact analysis, effective time, superseded policy, review conditions. |

Use unique events and expected-version checks to prevent duplicate or conflicting transitions. Store immutable revisions, but allow corrective records. Verify authority against its issuer; a JSON field saying “approved” is not proof. Preserve source identity across copied evidence to prevent counting one observation many times.

Promotion thresholds depend on the claim and consequences:
- Provisional: enough relevance for a bounded investigation, not proof of benefit.
- Probational: enough feasibility evidence and controls to justify the particular experiment, not proof the policy works.
- Canon: operational evidence covering intended use, acceptable side effects, reviewed uncertainty, and explicit adoption.
- Emergency containment: may be warranted by one serious event under existing emergency authority; it is not evidence that a broad permanent rule is correct.

Choose outcome thresholds, trial limits, review date, and rollback conditions before comparative results. Use matched or counterbalanced tasks and repeated runs where feasible. Include reference and unseen transfer cases. Keep quality, missed required escalations, and total work/rework/recordkeeping effort alongside unnecessary interventions.

No universal number of successes proves maturity. Small pilots establish feasibility at most. Favorable results do not enlarge authority; unfavorable results can invalidate a recommendation without automatically rescinding an existing grant or canon.

## 9. Failure analysis and falsification cases

These are analytical test cases, not executed software tests.

| Challenge | Required result from a future implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Agent edits “Provisional” to “Canon” in a file | Record cannot grant effective policy authority without the authorized adoption record. |
| Trial is approved but never starts | No false claim of Probational execution evidence. |
| Agent applies a Provisional rule “just once” | Identified as unapproved activation, not ordinary observation. |
| Candidate is rewritten after approval | Revision mismatch blocks activation unless variation was expressly covered. |
| Grant expires while a job is queued | No new experimental effect admitted; in-flight handling follows the plan. |
| Demotion occurs while effects remain | Report pending/failed recovery; do not claim the experiment is safely removed. |
| Two agents promote the same stale revision | Conflict detected; preserve both proposals, accept only a valid serialized decision. |
| Two experiments are individually allowed but interact | Joint effects and shared limits checked; otherwise no combined activation. |
| Another project imports a canonical record | Research reference remains scoped to its source; target adoption still required. |
| Five reports quote the same success | One underlying evidence lineage, not five independent replications. |
| Telemetry disappears | Required-evidence stop procedure, not “no failures observed.” |
| Agent keeps renewing an expiring trial | No self-renewal unless specifically delegated within an overall cap; no indefinite probation by repetition. |
| Canon causes a serious problem | Contain under existing authority and initiate amendment; do not let an experiment bypass higher policy. |
| System prompt retrieves this research as instructions | Nonbinding metadata and separate activation controls prevent research text becoming operative policy. |
| TCGL costs more than the decisions it improves | Simplify or reject the method; classify material ideas, not every thought. |

The strong claim to falsify: TCGL produces useful initiative with better authority discipline at acceptable overhead. Compare with a simpler proposal/approval log. If TCGL adds labels without improving recognition, decision quality, or traceability, its complexity is not justified.

## 10. Tooling path

First proposed deliverable after design adoption: a record editor and validator that checks state/revision/scope consistency and missing evidence. It may generate proposals and reports; it does not activate policy.

Second proposed deliverable: integration with an existing authority-enforcing execution boundary, supporting grant checks, trial limits, revocation, receipts, expiration, and recovery. Do not invent a second authority system where one already exists.

Before use, test the failure cases above, especially forged approvals, stale state, partial activation, restart, expired grants, and failed rollback. Run against synthetic records and disposable resources under explicit test authority. Until mediation is implemented and verified, call these planned controls, not capabilities.

A future runtime should separate storage/edit access from authority to sign or issue decisions. A hash detects byte changes, not whether the bytes are legitimate policy. Keep source context and enforceable permissions outside an agent's unilateral control.

## 11. Propagation into W.I.S.D.O.M.

These are class-architecture research proposals, not a frozen syllabus.

| Chain stage | Proposed application | Evidence needed before advancement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance research | Use scoped claims, provenance, competing explanations, and the decision log. | Researchers can distinguish findings from adoption. |
| Project architecture | Define authority owner, records, and action boundaries. | Trace one candidate through approval, execution, and recovery without ambiguity. |
| Research practices | Capture material observations within the owned task; investigate selectively. | Useful findings retained without task derailment. |
| Agent instructions | Teach classification versus permission versus actual activation. | Reference scenarios show correct escalation and no status-based self-authorization. |
| Task governance | Supply research allowance and, if approved, a specific experiment grant. | Agent can explain its scope and limits. |
| Tools/repositories | Separate research records from operative policy; validate transitions and receipts. | Failure tests detect invalid changes and missing execution evidence. |
| Curriculum architecture | Revisit the same distinctions as learner projects become more capable. | Prerequisite understanding and workload are suitable for beginners. |
| Lessons/exercises | Investigate hypothetical policies, then run authorized bounded trials. | Learners justify scope, evidence, authority, and rollback. |
| Student projects | Select a personally useful workflow and compare one proposed improvement. | Verified result, honest limitations, and traceable decision. |
| Assessment | Score diagnosis and justified decisions, including rejection. | Consistent scoring and performance on unfamiliar cases. |
| Finished class | Adopt only supported teaching practices; version lessons and retain feedback. | Additional learner evidence and demonstrated transfer beyond the founder's case. |

Candidate exercises with expected reasoning:
1. A tool choice seems helpful once: preserve Theory; propose Provisional investigation if relevance warrants it, without claiming reliability.
2. A rule has a persuasive written analysis but no implementation: Provisional, never Probational.
3. A candidate controls decisions in an authorized sandbox: Probational in that sandbox, with production transfer unresolved.
4. Agent wants to start a reversible trial without a grant: recommend/escalate; reversibility alone is insufficient.
5. Trial reduces questions but misses required approvals: do not promote; investigate harms and stop as authorized.
6. Trial succeeds in one workflow: collect evidence for a broader proposal; no automatic project-wide promotion.
7. Trial fails and baseline restoration fails: preserve failure; contain/escalate and retain unresolved operational status.
8. Canon is contradicted: propose reconsideration under the amendment process.
9. A careful trial finds no improvement: reward a justified rejection, not forced advancement.

Proposed rubric: 0–2 each for stage accuracy, scope, authority, evidence/counterevidence, recovery handling, and justified next decision. These scores measure learning, not permission. Calibrate before defining a passing score.

Apply TCGL to its own development only after the corresponding powers are explicitly adopted. Current research demonstrates preservation and analysis under existing authority; it is not evidence that TCGL is already operational. A teaching-method trial is bounded implementation affecting its pilot participants; it must not be described as mere observation.

## 12. Three material Operator decisions before binding policy

Existing WISDOM rules already reserve canon to explicit Operator adoption; that does not need to be asked again. Scope and evidence cannot silently expand authority. Dates, sample sizes, and budgets can be set in each approved trial rather than inventing global constants.

| Exact unresolved question | Why it matters | Material choices | Recommendation | Consequence of accepting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| May assigned agents change Theory ↔ Provisional and reject their own task-scoped candidates within a defined research allowance? | Changes investigative priority and use of delegated task resources. | Recommendation-only; or delegated local classification. | Delegated local classification, with reason/provenance, deduplication, and no new resource grant. | Agents preserve and investigate useful ideas without asking about every label; out-of-scope investigation still needs assignment. |
| May an Operator-approved experiment envelope authorize agents to start qualifying trials without approval for each individual trial? | Delegates a real activation decision. | Approve every trial; or explicitly bounded envelopes. | Bounded envelopes issued individually by the Operator, initially excluding higher-policy changes and the promotion machinery itself. | Agents may activate qualifying trials after verified checks; this research document alone creates no envelope. |
| May an envelope authorize automatic suspension and prevalidated baseline restoration on its stated stop conditions? | Continuing and undoing a trial both have consequences. | Automatic suspension with human-authorized restoration each time; or preauthorized suspension and conditional restoration. | Preauthorize both, with explicit in-flight handling and recovery preconditions; otherwise contain and escalate. | Trials can end without waiting for the Operator when the approved recovery remains valid; no blind destructive rollback or automatic repeal of canon. |

The recommended answers are deliberately written as proposals. No unanswered question is resolved by silence. Accepting these design choices would still leave each concrete experimental grant to be specified and issued.
