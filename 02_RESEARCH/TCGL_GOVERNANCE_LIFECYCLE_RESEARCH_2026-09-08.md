## User-defined meanings

THEORY → PROVISIONAL → PROBATIONAL → CANON, with governed backward movement.

- Theory: an idea being reasoned about; no implementation status or authority.
- Provisional: sufficiently promising for deliberate investigation of consequences, applications, risks, conflicts and implementation possibilities; not implemented.
- Probational: deliberately implemented in a bounded experiment, with observation of actual behavior; not permanent.
- Canon: durable governing policy supported by evidence and authorized approval, amendable through the governing process.

## Initial findings to preserve

1. Separate knowledge classification, grant of experimental authority, and receipt of actual activation. Approval to start is not proof the trial started.
2. Provisional investigation consumes task time and compute even without operational policy authority; autonomous promotion needs a research budget/scope boundary.
3. Demotion is a knowledge decision; stopping or rolling back a live effect is an operational action. Neither should silently stand in for the other.
4. Maturity must attach to an exact principle revision and scope. Evidence and grants cannot silently transfer to rewritten principles or broader scopes.
5. Automatic expiration should terminate experimental permission under a defined protocol; it need not claim the underlying theory is false.
6. Canonical authority and empirical truth are different. Negative evidence initiates governed reconsideration; agents cannot unilaterally repeal canon.
