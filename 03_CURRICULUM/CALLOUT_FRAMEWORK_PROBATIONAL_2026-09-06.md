# Recurring Callout Framework — Probational

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Status:** PROBATIONAL
**Area:** instructional presentation / embedded evidence
**Authority:** approved for probationary use and evaluation

## Design constraint

Use the minimum number of recurring callout families necessary to create a stable visual and cognitive language.

A new family should not be added merely because a new label sounds useful. Its first burden is to show that its function cannot be expressed clearly through the existing families.

## Current minimal framework

### 1. Connection

Purpose: make relationships between ideas visible at the moment the relationship becomes useful.

**Connection is the umbrella.** Convergence and causal-chain tracing are modes of Connection, not separate callout families.

A Connection may therefore show:

- a direct link between a current concept and prior learning;
- a **convergence**, where several previously separate ideas combine into a larger system or understanding;
- a **causal trace**, where the learner follows cause → consequence → evidence.

The causal mode should ask what caused the result, what changed because of it, and what evidence actually supports the claimed relationship.

### 2. Field Check

Purpose: create a zero-pressure retrieval, prediction, explanation, or recognition opportunity before immediate feedback.

A Field Check:

- is not graded;
- is not a status marker;
- does not punish an incorrect answer;
- gives the learner a brief chance to think before the explanation is revealed;
- exists to strengthen learning and expose misunderstanding, not to judge the learner.

### 3. Principle in Action

Purpose: reinforce a foundational or constitutional principle precisely when an actual event gives the principle meaning.

Likely uses include:

- delegated authority;
- evidence versus assertion;
- explicit adoption;
- policy versus execution;
- unnecessary escalation;
- governance branching or overconstraint;
- learner autonomy and the limits of instructional authority.

This is the primary constitutional reinforcement family.

## Why Connection absorbs Convergence and Causal Chain

Separating all three would create overlapping labels for the same fundamental instructional act: **making the structure of relationships visible**.

The modes remain available because they describe different shapes of relationship. They do not currently justify separate visual families.

## Probation questions

During use, test whether:

- learners can distinguish the three families without explanation;
- callouts improve recognition, retrieval, transfer, or understanding;
- Principle in Action reinforces constitutional thinking without becoming preachy or interruptive;
- Connection becomes too broad to remain useful;
- causal traces need a distinct treatment after real use;
- the frequency of callouts creates visual bureaucracy;
- the framework can be reduced further.

If a family does not earn its keep, demote, merge, or remove it.
