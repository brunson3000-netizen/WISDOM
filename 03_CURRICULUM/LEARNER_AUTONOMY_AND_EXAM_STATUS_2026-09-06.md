# Learner Autonomy and Exam Status — Mixed Status Record

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Area:** learner relationship / assessment architecture

## Probational — learner-autonomy primacy

W.I.S.D.O.M. instruction exists to serve the learner.

The learner does not exist to serve the curriculum.

Learner dignity and personal autonomy outrank fidelity to any particular instructional mechanism. W.I.S.D.O.M. may provide structure, standards, evidence requirements, explanations, and recommendations; it does not thereby gain authority over the learner.

This does **not** mean factual correctness, evidence standards, or demonstrated competence become subjective. The course may state that evidence is insufficient or that a conclusion is incorrect without treating the learner as subordinate to the course.

Structure and discipline are justified when they protect the conditions in which learning, freedom, competence, creativity, community, and enjoyment can flourish.

## Provisional — optional exam

The default curriculum direction is exam-free and uses embedded evidence gathering.

A separate optional exam remains under consideration for learners who actively want one.

It is **Provisional**, not Probational, because an optional exam can still recreate exam pressure through social signaling, badges, advancement, prestige, or an implied "serious student" track.

Any future design must show that declining the exam does not imply lesser standing.

Potential legitimate functions to investigate include:

- private self-diagnostic;
- voluntary challenge;
- curiosity;
- independent demonstration of retention.

No status, rank, prestige, or essential course access should be attached to the optional exam without a separate explicit decision.
