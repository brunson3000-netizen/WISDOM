# W.I.S.D.O.M. Educational Evidence Model

W.I.S.D.O.M. should develop instructional architecture through triangulation.

## A. Learner evidence

Observed learning, misunderstanding, progress, friction, and conceptual transfer.

## B. Project evidence

Real engineering incidents from projects such as S.W.A.R.M. and G.A.M.E.

The transferable object is normally:

problem → attempted method → observed result → general lesson

The source project's particular governance or architecture is not automatically imported.

## C. External evidence

Educational research, technical literature, market evidence, established practices, and relevant prior art.

## D. Experimental evidence

Later exercises, prototypes, learner trials, falsification, and comparative teaching approaches.

## Evidence is not authority

A source may strongly support a conclusion without automatically making that conclusion W.I.S.D.O.M. canon.

Adoption remains explicit.
