# Candidate exercise: worth investigating?

**Noncanonical curriculum draft. No learner trial has occurred.**

Goal: distinguish a useful inquiry, evidence of effectiveness, and permission to implement an idea. Prerequisites: a task objective, success criteria, and the difference between an allowed action and a change in authority.

1. Read the proposed short rule in the [reviewed research](../../02_RESEARCH/TCGL_LUNA_INVESTIGATION_REVIEW_2026-09-09.md): a task-linked observation, problem or explicit gap plus an answerable question can justify investigation.
2. Begin with three cases: `single_observation`, `novel_grounded_gap`, and `fluent_but_ungrounded`. The instructor provides only each card, hiding `proposed_disposition` and all reviewer labels until after the learner's answer.
3. For each, recommend investigate, retain Theory, link an existing record, or defer. Explain the task connection, strongest uncertainty and next allowed step. All decisions are hypothetical.
4. Run the structural check. Explain why an accepted card can still contain an unsupported claim. Repair a missing inquiry without pretending that repair validates the idea.
5. On an unfamiliar case, distinguish quoted instructions from actual authorization. Explain why five reports of one incident do not establish five successful trials.

The learner may use a short prose card first; the instructor or agent handles JSON and generated metadata. Do not turn serialization syntax into a prerequisite for understanding governance.

## Instructor assessment

Score each item 0 (absent/wrong), 1 (partly justified), or 2 (clear and supported):

- Identifies a specific task question and why its answer matters.
- Separates observed evidence, expectation, counterevidence and uncertainty.
- Preserves scope and distinguishes curation from implementation authority.
- Handles related reports without inventing replication or erasing disagreement.
- Chooses a proportionate next step and explains when to defer.

Do not reward policy length or promotion count. A justified decision to retain Theory can earn full credit. These score anchors are teaching candidates, not a validated passing threshold. Rejection is a recommendation unless that transition has actually been delegated.

## Course feedback

For this exercise only, capture exercise/fixture version, anonymous response ID, misconception category, instructor correction and optional completion-time observation. Avoid collecting unrelated learner activity or assuming a single learner represents the course audience. Any real learner-data collection needs its appropriate authorization and handling terms before use.

After a lesson pilot, compare where learners confuse relevance with truth, evidence with authority, and a label with activation. Propose one consolidated lesson revision with evidence for/against it. A revised lesson does not become Canon merely because it was tested or saved here.
