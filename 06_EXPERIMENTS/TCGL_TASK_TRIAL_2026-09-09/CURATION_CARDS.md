## P-01 — Agent observations as diagnostic leads

**Record:** P-01/r1 · **Source status:** Theory · **Current status:** Provisional, task-local  
**Source:** [integrated report §7 and G-06](https://github.com/brunson3000-netizen/WISDOM/blob/8e9afa836e09448ca347a6e7200ae502e6e10846/02_RESEARCH/GAB_TCGL_INTEGRATED_RESEARCH_2026-09-08.md). Original hypothesis: distributed agent observations can improve governance research.

**Why investigate:** this task needs relevant governance findings without a general audit. Agent-produced reports already contain diagnostic leads, but their evidentiary strength varies.

**Question and authorized inquiry:** which issues in the saved offline review have an execution-result anchor, and which have only a reported interpretation? Compare the existing report and receipt; do not commission more observers.

**Expected effects:** quicker retrieval of useful leads; risk of copied explanations, omissions and confident self-justification being treated as findings.

**Evidence for/against and uncertainty:** the saved offline review identifies concrete timestamp/revision defects; the final 18-test receipt supports properties of the corrected build. That receipt does not reproduce the initial failing run or establish completeness of the observer's report. No independent observation-quality or cross-model comparison exists here.

**Negation and current inquiry result:** “agent reports are reliable or comprehensive sensors” does not follow. Classify reported defects as reported history unless the corresponding execution evidence is available. The narrower proposition—selective reports may help diagnosis when their support is visible—remains worth investigating.

**Scope / next useful question:** this task's diagnostic record only. Which material incident needs a direct execution reference before a later reviewer relies on its causal account? No broader reporting requirement is adopted.

**Relations:** P-03 addresses duplicated evidence; it is not a second validation of P-01.

## P-02 — A compact card with shared provenance

**Record:** P-02/r1 · **Source status:** Theory design proposal · **Current status:** Provisional, task-local  
**Sources:** [integrated report §8](https://github.com/brunson3000-netizen/WISDOM/blob/8e9afa836e09448ca347a6e7200ae502e6e10846/02_RESEARCH/GAB_TCGL_INTEGRATED_RESEARCH_2026-09-08.md) and [raw Luna “Finding first”](https://github.com/brunson3000-netizen/WISDOM/blob/8e9afa836e09448ca347a6e7200ae502e6e10846/02_RESEARCH/TCGL_LUNA_RAW_INVESTIGATION_2026-09-09.md). Original proposal: short cards with provenance; Luna additionally called its mechanism “the smallest useful.”

**Why investigate:** the mechanism intended to reduce supervision also creates reading and recording work.

**Question and authorized inquiry:** what information in the proposed card could be removed or shared without losing the reason for investigation, the task boundary or the evidence trail? Perform a documentary omission analysis, not a new operational change.

**Expected effects:** less repeated context reconstruction; risk of overcompression hiding uncertainty or inaccessible inherited context.

**Evidence for/against and uncertainty:** the parent review reduced and grouped the raw proposal's many required fields; the prototype generates identity metadata. Neither establishes minimum size, beginner usability or reduced human burden. A simple prose note could outperform the structured card for some tasks.

**Negation and current inquiry result:** discard “smallest” as an established conclusion. In a conceptual omission analysis, removing the task anchor loses relevance; removing the question loses the purpose of attention; removing evidence/uncertainty blurs observation and expectation; removing the scope/grant link hides limits. Shared task/actor/version references can avoid repetition when still accessible. These are design arguments, not measured user results.

**Scope / next useful question:** this task's existing card design only. Compare a short prose note with the card on whether a reviewer can recover those four elements and how much reading each requires. No learner experiment or new runtime recording rule is activated.

**Relations:** the T2P-CARD-V1 trial uses the already-approved card. This hypothesis investigates its economy; it does not authorize revising that live trial mid-pass.

## P-03 — Source-event identity without false replication

**Record:** P-03/r1 · **Source status:** Theory mechanism proposal · **Current status:** Provisional, task-local  
**Source:** [integrated report §§7–8 and proposed deduplication tooling in §12](https://github.com/brunson3000-netizen/WISDOM/blob/8e9afa836e09448ca347a6e7200ae502e6e10846/02_RESEARCH/GAB_TCGL_INTEGRATED_RESEARCH_2026-09-08.md).

**Why investigate:** synthesis can make one incident appear more persuasive when several reports repeat it.

**Question and authorized inquiry:** can the saved evidence distinguish reports from underlying events, and where does exact matching stop helping? Inspect the existing retrieval receipt and the offline review's repeated timestamp-defect account.

**Expected effects:** avoid counting the same identified event twice; risk of missed aliases, incorrectly shared IDs and false confidence in semantic deduplication.

**Evidence for/against and uncertainty:** the [prior synthetic receipt](https://github.com/brunson3000-netizen/WISDOM/blob/8e9afa836e09448ca347a6e7200ae502e6e10846/06_EXPERIMENTS/TCGL_OFFLINE_V1/RESULTS.json) groups two reports under one supplied E-1 identifier. The offline review mentions the timestamp defect in both its narrative and issue table; these are two accounts of the same reported episode, not new independent failures. The original failing trace is not part of the supplied receipt, so no new event details are inferred.

**Negation and current inquiry result:** exact identifiers do not discover whether differently named incidents are the same event, nor establish causal independence. Restrict the mechanism's demonstrated claim to grouping supplied equal IDs. Preserve alternative explanations and uncertain event matches.

**Scope / next useful question:** this task's evidence attribution only. In a future authorized evaluation, test different IDs for the same reported event and the same ID mistakenly assigned to different events; measure reconciliation errors separately from exact-key matching. That evaluation is not launched by this card.

**Relations:** P-01 concerns usefulness of reports; P-03 concerns their attribution. Both reference the same saved material, not independent replication.

## Selection account
