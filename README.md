# W.I.S.D.O.M.

W.I.S.D.O.M. is an educational project for teaching people how to work effectively with AI through disciplined, evidence-informed methods.

The project is centered on a premium beginner learning experience for people who want to use AI to accomplish their own goals. It is not primarily a coding bootcamp or conventional career-training program, although some learners may pursue professional or engineering goals.

## Core approach

W.I.S.D.O.M. develops instruction from:

- tested engineering practices;
- AI interaction and inference concepts;
- project setup and disciplined workflow;
- governance, policy, and constitutional design where educationally useful;
- practical exercises;
- evidence gathered from real AI-assisted project work;
- research into learner needs and market demand.

The project must distinguish clearly between:

- established project canon;
- researched findings;
- curriculum under development;
- experimental ideas;
- provisional theory;
- marketing hypotheses.

Brainstorming does not become canon merely because it was discussed.
